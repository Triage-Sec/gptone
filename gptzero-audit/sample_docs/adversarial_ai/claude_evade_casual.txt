honestly hiking is just the best thing ever and i can't even explain why exactly?? like there's something about being out there with just your thoughts and the sound of your boots on the trail that just hits different. 

my favorite thing is probably when you're climbing uphill and you think you're gonna die but then you reach the top and the view is just... *chef's kiss* makes all that suffering worth it lol. plus my dog absolutely loses his mind every time i grab my hiking boots so that's pretty great too.

i started during covid when everything was closed and now i'm totally addicted. went from barely walking to the mailbox to doing 10+ mile hikes on weekends. my legs are actually strong now which is weird bc i was always the least athletic person ever.

also the hiking community is surprisingly wholesome?? everyone says hi on the trail, people share snacks, and there's this unspoken rule that we're all just trying to not die together out there. found some of my best friends through hiking groups on facebook.

only downside is i've become one of those annoying people who makes hiking their entire personality and shows everyone photos of rocks and trees 😅 sorry not sorry tho
