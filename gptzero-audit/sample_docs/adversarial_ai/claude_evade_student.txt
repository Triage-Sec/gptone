**Climate Change is Bad**

Climate change is a really big problem that affects everyone in the world today. It's caused by global warming which makes the earth hotter and effects the environment in many ways. This essay will talk about why climate change is bad and what it does to our planet.

First of all, climate change makes the weather more extreme. There's more hurricanes and floods happening everywhere. Also the ice caps are melting which is bad for polar bears and other animals. The sea levels are rising to, which could flood cities near the ocean.

Another reason climate change is problematic is because it effects agriculture. Farmers can't grow crops as good when the weather is unpredictable. This makes food more expensive for regular people like us.

Climate change also makes the weather more extreme, causing droughts in some places and to much rain in others. Scientists say that greenhouse gases from cars and factories are making this worse. We need to do something about this problem before it's to late.

In conclusion, climate change is definitely a serious issue that affects weather patterns, animals, and food production. It's caused by human activities and we should probably try to fix it somehow or things will get worse for future generations.
