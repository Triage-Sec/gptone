# My First Week of Intermittent Fasting: What Actually Happened

So I finally caved and tried intermittent fasting. I know, I know – I'm probably three years late to this party, but better late than never, right?

I went with the 16:8 method, which basically means I eat during an 8-hour window and fast for 16 hours. Sounds dramatic, but it's really just skipping breakfast and eating between noon and 8 PM.

The first two days were... rough. I'm usually a "coffee and toast at 7 AM" person, so my stomach was NOT having it. I felt like a cranky toddler by 11 AM, and my coworkers probably thought I'd lost my mind.

But here's the weird part – by day four, I actually felt pretty good. My afternoon energy crashes disappeared, and I wasn't constantly thinking about snacks. Who knew?

The hardest part wasn't the hunger (though that sucked initially). It was the social aspect. Turns out, a lot of my social life revolves around food, and explaining why I couldn't grab brunch got old fast.

Will I keep doing it? Maybe a few days a week. It's not magic, but it did make me more mindful about when and why I eat.
