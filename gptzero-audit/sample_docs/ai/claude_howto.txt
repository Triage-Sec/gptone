# How to Start Composting at Home

## Step 1: Choose Your Method
Select either a compost bin, tumbler, or simple pile in your yard. For apartments, consider a small indoor composter or worm bin.

## Step 2: Find the Right Location
Place your composter in a partially shaded area with good drainage and easy access to water.

## Step 3: Gather Materials
Collect "greens" (nitrogen-rich): fruit scraps, vegetable peels, coffee grounds, fresh grass clippings
Collect "browns" (carbon-rich): dry leaves, newspaper, cardboard, twigs

## Step 4: Layer Your Materials
Start with browns at the bottom, then alternate layers of greens and browns in a 3:1 ratio (more browns than greens).

## Step 5: Add Water
Lightly moisten each layer—the pile should feel like a wrung-out sponge.

## Step 6: Turn Regularly
Mix or turn your compost every 2-3 weeks to add oxygen and speed decomposition.

## Step 7: Monitor and Maintain
Keep the pile moist but not soggy. Add materials regularly while maintaining the proper ratio.

## Step 8: Harvest
After 3-6 months, you'll have dark, crumbly compost ready for your garden!

**Avoid:** Meat, dairy, oils, pet waste, and diseased plants.
