The day I lost Max feels like it was just yesterday, yet somehow it haunts me like a ghost I can’t shake. He was more than just a dog; he was my confidant, my shadow, my silly little buddy who never judged me. I still remember that morning, the way the sun seeped through the curtains, casting golden light over his bed. I thought he was just napping, but deep down, I knew. 

When I knelt beside him, the weight of the world settled on my shoulders. His fur was warm, but his eyes, oh those big, brown eyes, didn’t light up like they used to. I wrapped my arms around him, and it felt like saying goodbye to a piece of my heart. I whispered everything I needed him to hear, all the love and laughter we shared, and all the days I could have shown him more. 

The silence in my house now is deafening. His toys sit untouched, a heart-wrenching reminder of the moments we’ll never share again. I wish I could have one more walk, one more belly rub, one more moment. I guess all I can do is hold onto those memories, even if they sting a little more each day.
